How I work

I came to software from Chemical Physics and classroom teaching. That background decides how I work: measure the output instead of trusting the report. Every system here runs a check on its own result before it hands it over.

I have also killed a product for exactly that reason. A lead-generation service I built began producing contacts that could not be verified — the model was inventing them — so I shut it down rather than sell unverified leads. Knowing when an AI output is not trustworthy is most of the work.
